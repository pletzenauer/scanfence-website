## Compare the plans

All plans include geofencing, multi-location codes, time-based routing, scan limits and analytics. They differ in size.

| | Basic | Starter | Professional |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly | $4.99 | $14.99 | $44.99 |
| Annual | $49 | $149 | $449 |
| QR codes | 100 | 500 | 2,500 |
| Geofences | 100 | 500 | 2,500 |
| Scans per month | 25,000 | 125,000 | 1,250,000 |
| Team seats (including you) | 2 | 3 | 10 |
| Free trial | 30 days | 30 days | 30 days |

Current prices are always on the [pricing page](/pricing/).

## The free trial

Every plan starts with 30 days free. You enter your card at checkout, but nothing is charged until the trial ends. Cancel before then in the billing portal and you pay nothing. If you already have a running subscription, a plan change starts without a new trial.

## Upgrade, downgrade or cancel

Open **My Settings** and click <kbd>Open billing portal →</kbd>. There you can change your plan, update your card, download invoices and cancel. See [Settings and billing](/documentation/account-settings/).

## When you reach a limit

- **QR codes.** The QR Codes page shows how many you've used, for example *7 / 100 codes*. When you reach your plan's limit, <kbd>+ Generate QR</kbd> is switched off until you delete a code or upgrade. Codes in the trash don't count.
- **Team seats.** On the Team page you'll see *Seat limit reached* and can't send new invitations until you free a seat or upgrade.

## Without a plan

If your subscription ends, you can still sign in, see your dashboard, change settings and contact support. Geofences, QR codes, analytics, team and the audit log stay locked until you pick a plan again. Click **View plans** on the dashboard banner.
