## Plan and billing

The tiles at the top show your **plan** and its status, how many **QR codes** and **scans per month** it includes, your team seats, and since when you're a member. The **Subscription** box lists everything your plan includes.

- <kbd>Upgrade plan →</kbd> shows the plans side by side.
- <kbd>Open billing portal →</kbd> or <kbd>Billing portal</kbd> takes you to our payment provider's secure portal. There you change or cancel your plan, update your card and download invoices.

See [Plans and limits](/documentation/plans-and-limits/) for what each plan includes.

## Profile

The **Profile** box shows your email, name, role and user ID. Quote your user ID when you contact support; it helps us find your account quickly.

## Routing context

Under **Location & time**, pick your **Timezone** and, optionally, a **Default location** such as *Vienna, Austria*. Click <kbd>Save settings →</kbd>.

## Change your password

Under **Security**, enter a **New password** twice (at least 6 characters) and click <kbd>Change password →</kbd>.

Forgot your password? On the sign-in page click **Forgot password?**, enter your email and follow the link we send you.

## Tags

Create, rename, recolour and delete the tags you use to label codes. See [Categories and tags](/documentation/manage-qr-codes/#categories-and-tags).

## Light or dark

Switch between **Mission · dark** and **Instrument · light** at the bottom of the menu. The app remembers your choice on this device.

## Delete your account

In the **Danger zone**, click <kbd>Delete my account →</kbd>. The window lists everything that will be removed. Type **DELETE** to confirm.

<div class="warn"><strong>This can't be undone.</strong> Everything listed in the window is removed, and your codes stop working. Cancel your subscription in the billing portal first, and download any exports you want to keep.</div>
