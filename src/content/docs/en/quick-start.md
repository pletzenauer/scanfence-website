## 1. Create your account

1. Go to [app.scanfence.com/signup](https://app.scanfence.com/signup).
2. Pick a plan: **Basic**, **Starter** or **Professional**. Switch between **Monthly** and **Annual** billing at the top; annual saves about 17 %. Every plan starts with a 30-day free trial. See [Plans and limits](/documentation/plans-and-limits/) if you're unsure which to take.
3. Enter your work email and a password (at least 6 characters), confirm it, and accept the Privacy Policy and Terms.
4. Have a voucher? Enter it in **Voucher code** before you continue.
5. Click **Create account & subscribe**. You're taken to our payment provider to enter your card, then back to the app.

> If you're asked to confirm your email first, click the link in the email and sign in. Checkout carries on where you left off.

## 2. Create your first code

1. In the menu, open **QR Codes** and click <kbd>+ Generate QR</kbd>.
2. Leave the type on **Standard**.
3. Give the code a name, for example *Window poster*. Names show up in lists, analytics and exports.
4. Enter the web address people should land on in **URL or text**.
5. Tick **Make this a dynamic QR code**. You can then change the address later without reprinting.
6. Click <kbd>Generate QR →</kbd>.

## 3. Test and download it

1. Your new code appears as a card at the top of the list. Point your phone's camera at the screen: it should open your address.
2. Click <kbd>PNG</kbd> for a picture you can drop into documents, or <kbd>SVG</kbd> for print. SVG stays sharp at any size, so send that one to your printer.
3. After a few scans, the card's **Scans & limits** box counts them and <kbd>View analytics</kbd> shows where they came from.
