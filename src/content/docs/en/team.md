title: Team and roles
description: Invite colleagues into your workspace and decide what each of them may do.
---

Everyone you invite joins your workspace and works with the same codes and zones, within the limits of their role. Team members use your plan; they don't need their own subscription.

## Roles

| Role | Can do | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| **Admin** | Everything: all codes of all users, team management, analytics, settings. | Owners and team leads. |
| **Manager** | Create, edit and delete QR codes, view analytics, invite people. | Marketing, venue or campaign managers. |
| **Field user** | See and download codes and use them on site. Can't create, change or delete codes. | Staff who only work with printed codes. |

## Invite someone

1. Open **Team** and click <kbd>+ Invite member</kbd>.
2. Enter their **Email address**.
3. Choose a **Role**. The box underneath explains what it allows.
4. Click <kbd>Send invitation →</kbd>.

They get an email with a link. Invitations are valid for **7 days**. Until then they're listed under pending invitations, where you can **cancel** them.

Each plan has a number of seats, and you count as one. The **Seats used** tile shows how many are taken. When they're all used, you'll see *Seat limit reached*; remove someone or [upgrade](/documentation/plans-and-limits/).

## Remove someone

Click **Remove** next to the person and confirm. They lose access to the workspace immediately. You can't remove yourself.

## Joining a team

If someone invited you:

1. Open the link in the invitation email.
2. **New to ScanFence?** Choose **Create account**, enter your full name and a password, accept the terms and click <kbd>Create account & join team →</kbd>. Your email is already filled in from the invitation.
3. **Already have an account?** Choose **Sign in** and click <kbd>Sign in & join team →</kbd>.
4. If you're already signed in, click <kbd>Accept invitation →</kbd>, or **Decline**.

Pending invitations also show on your [dashboard](/documentation/dashboard/) with a <kbd>Review</kbd> button.

> **"This invitation was sent to a different email address"** means you're signed in with another account. Sign out, then open the link again.
>
> **"Invitation not found or has expired"** means the 7 days are over or the invitation was cancelled. Ask for a new one.
