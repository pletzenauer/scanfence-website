ScanFence makes QR codes you print once and control forever. A code can send people to a different page depending on **where** they scan it (geofences), **when** they scan it (time-based rules) and **how many** people scanned before them (scan limits). You can change all of this later without reprinting anything.

## The app at a glance

The menu on the left of the app has these sections:

| Section | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Today's numbers and a live feed of the latest scans. |
| **Geofences** | Create and edit the zones your codes check against. |
| **QR Codes** | Create, download, edit, filter and delete your codes. |
| **Analytics** | Maps, charts and CSV exports of your scans. |
| **Team** | Invite people and manage their roles. |
| **Audit Log** | A record of every change made in your workspace. |
| **Support** | Contact options and hands-on help sessions. |
| **My Settings** | Your plan, billing, password, tags and account deletion. |

At the bottom of the menu you can switch between the dark theme (**Mission**) and the light theme (**Instrument**), and sign out. On a phone the menu sits behind the ☰ button in the top bar.

> **Try before you sign up.** The [live demo](/demo/app/) is the real app running on sample data. Click anything you like; nothing is saved and it resets when you reload.

## Key ideas in one minute

- **Static or dynamic.** A *static* code contains your link directly: fast and simple, but you can never change it and it isn't counted. A *dynamic* code points to ScanFence, which forwards the scanner. That's what lets you edit the destination, count scans and add rules later. [More](/documentation/create-qr-codes/#static-or-dynamic)
- **Geofence.** A circle on the map with a radius between 50 m and 5 km. A geofence code only works for people standing inside it. [More](/documentation/geofences/)
- **Workspace.** Everything you create belongs to your organisation's workspace. Colleagues you invite share it according to their role. [More](/documentation/team/)
