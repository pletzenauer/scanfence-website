The audit log records every time someone in your workspace creates, changes or deletes something: QR codes, geofences, team members and invitations, subscriptions, categories and tags. Use it to answer "who changed the menu link on Friday?" or to show an auditor how your codes are managed.

## Reading the list

Each row shows **when** it happened, **who** did it, the **action** (*Insert* for new, *Update* for changed, *Delete* for removed), the **table** it affected, and a short summary such as *3 fields changed*.

**Click a row** to expand it. You'll see each changed field with its old and new value side by side.

## Finding an entry

- **Search** by user, table or action.
- **Action:** only inserts, updates or deletes.
- **Table:** only QR codes, geofences, user profiles, team invitations, subscriptions, categories or tags.
- **From / To:** a date range.
- **Clear filters** resets everything.

The page shows the latest 500 matching events. Narrow the filters to reach older ones.

## Export

<kbd>Export CSV →</kbd> downloads the filtered entries with timestamp, user email, action, table, record ID and the old and new data.
